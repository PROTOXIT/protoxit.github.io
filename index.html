Legal & Company Timeline

From Proprietorship to DAO

Our legal and structural evolution is as important as our trading technology. Here is the timeline of PROTOXIT's corporate and legal journey.

Company timeline

2020
Founding & Proprietorship
Samuel J. Greenberg founded PROTOXIT as a proprietary trading firm, focusing on crypto arbitrage and quantitative strategies.
2020 – 2026
Growth under Proprietorship
The company operated as a proprietorship, expanding its trading infrastructure and team across global markets.
2026
DAO Formation to Enter the Exchange Business
PROTOXIT is creating a DAO (Decentralized Autonomous Organization) specifically to launch and operate a crypto exchange business — a new vertical alongside our established trading operations. The exchange will serve retail and institutional users across spot and derivatives markets.

New DAO structure: The DAO will govern the exchange business, enabling decentralized decision-making and broader community participation in exchange operations and listings.

Legal jurisdiction search: The company is actively identifying suitable legal jurisdictions that support DAO operations and crypto-friendly regulation for the exchange.

Existing business: PROTOXIT's trading business (arbitrage, futures, and options) continues to operate as before under the proprietorship structure.

Coming soon: PROTOXIT token, stablecoin, and futures & options exchange — details to be announced.
